Trillion-parameter-class output from 5.1B active parameters

Ling 3.0 Flash is InclusionAI (Ant Group)’s 124B-total hybrid-reasoning mixture-of-experts model with just 5.1B parameters active per token — about 8% of the network firing on any step. Announced July 24, 2026 and open-sourced under MIT on August 7, it matches or outperforms Ant’s own trillion-parameter Ring-2.6-1T flagship on most benchmarks while using roughly 12% of its total parameters. The architecture mixes Kimi Delta Attention and Multi-head Latent Attention layers in a 5:1 stack with a native hybrid instant/thinking mode, and the model card reports 56.6% on SWE-Bench Pro, 72.4% on SWE-Bench Multilingual, 93.2 on MathArena AIME 2026, and 22.7 on HLE, with a claimed 60-80% cut in time-to-first-token for long inputs via hierarchical caching.

Who it's for: Builders running production-scale agentic workloads who want open-weight control at flash-class serving cost.
