Chat your video edits into existence

Gemini Omni Flash, released in public preview June 30, 2026, is Google's new multimodal model for video creation and editing - the first of the Gemini Omni family, handling text, images, audio, and video natively without separate encoders. The model is being called the Nano Banana for video: you edit through conversation, telling it to make the violin invisible or change the sky to sunset, and it finds the object and changes it while keeping the rest of the shot smooth. It is available as gemini-omni-flash-preview in the Gemini API, with a stable gemini-omni-1.1-flash version updated through August 2026.

Who it's for: Creators and developers doing iterative video editing and generation who want conversation-driven control over shots

Key features

🎬 Conversational video editing

Refine and edit shots through natural-language dialogue using the Interactions API - ask for a retouch and everything else stays untouched

🎨 Object-aware edits

Finds the object you mention and changes it while keeping the rest of the video consistent - understands lighting and depth so new items fit the scene

⚡ Fast video generation

High-performance text-and-image to video generation, with 3-10 second output at up to 4K 24FPS in the stable 1.1 version

🔁 Iterative scene memory

The model remembers your scene across turns - change lighting or swap characters while keeping the video consistent

📦 1M-token multimodal context

1,048,576-token context window accepting text, image, and up to 10 seconds of video input for editing and extension

🌐 Free where creators already are

Free inside YouTube Shorts and the YouTube Create app; paid access via Gemini subscription or developer API from about $0.10 per second in 720p

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Editing by conversation is a genuine workflow change vs timeline scrubbing
  • Scene consistency across iterative edits
  • Free access path via YouTube Create
  • Stable gemini-omni-1.1-flash version already shipping

✗ What doesn't

  • Preview status - behavior, pricing, and availability may change
  • 10-second video input cap limits long-form editing
  • Requires a Gemini subscription or paid API outside YouTube products

Verdict

The most interesting video model of mid-2026 for anyone who edits more than they generate. Preview caveats apply, but conversational editing with scene memory is the closest thing to a Nano Banana moment for video.
