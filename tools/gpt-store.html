The App Store for AI agents

The GPT Store is OpenAI's marketplace where anyone can discover, use, and build custom GPTs — specialized AI agents for specific tasks. Need a GPT that only does legal contract review? There's one. A GPT that turns spreadsheets into charts? Built. A GPT that roleplays as your ideal customer for sales practice? You'll find 50 of them. With over 3 million custom GPTs in 2026, it's the largest AI agent marketplace.

Who it's for: Anyone who uses ChatGPT and wants specialized AI agents for specific tasks. Also for creators who want to build and monetize their own GPTs.

Key features

🔍 3M+ custom GPTs

Browse a marketplace of over 3 million specialized AI agents built for every conceivable use case — from logo design to tax prep.

🔨 No-code builder

Build your own GPT in minutes with plain English instructions. No programming needed — describe what you want and OpenAI handles the rest.

💰 Revenue sharing

Creators earn revenue when their GPTs are used. Top GPTs earn thousands per month from usage-based payouts.

🔌 Action integrations

GPTs can call external APIs — booking flights, querying databases, sending emails. They're not just chatbots; they're agents that act.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Massive ecosystem — 3M+ GPTs covering almost everything
  • Building a custom GPT requires zero code
  • Revenue sharing for creators is a real income stream
  • Free to browse and use many GPTs with ChatGPT subscription
  • Action/API integrations make GPTs genuinely useful agents

✗ What doesn't

  • Quality varies wildly — most GPTs are low-effort wrappers
  • Discovery is poor — finding the good ones requires trial and error
  • Revenue sharing is opaque and unpredictable
  • GPTs can't truly run autonomously — still need human prompts
  • Locked into OpenAI's ecosystem; no cross-platform portability

Verdict

The GPT Store is the closest thing to an "App Store for AI" in 2026. For users, it's worth browsing for specialized agents that outperform vanilla ChatGPT on specific tasks. For creators, building a useful GPT can be a genuine side income stream. The platform needs better discovery and quality control, but the foundation is strong.
