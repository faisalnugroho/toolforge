The image editing model that broke the internet

Nano Banana is the codename for Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash Image model, which went viral in August 2025 for its uncanny ability to edit photos while preserving subject identity. Tell it to put a person in a different setting, change their outfit, or place them in a group shot, and it returns results that look like real photos — not obvious AI composites. The model is available in the Gemini app, Google AI Studio, and via API, making it the first image editor serious enough for professional use cases.

Who it's for: E-commerce sellers editing product photos at scale, Content creators producing consistent character series, Marketers generating ad variations quickly, Anyone needing pro-quality photo edits without Photoshop
