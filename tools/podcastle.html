Record, edit, and ship podcasts without leaving your browser

Podcastle is the all-in-one podcast creation platform that lives in your browser. No software to install, no complex DAW to learn. You record remote interviews, edit with AI-assisted tools, add music, and publish to all major platforms — all from a single tab. In 2026, it's the fastest way to go from idea to published episode.

Who it's for: Podcasters, content creators, journalists, and educators who want professional-quality audio without the technical overhead of traditional podcasting setups.

Key features

Magic Dust AI noise removal

One-click AI enhancement that removes background noise, echo, and room reverb. Makes a $20 mic sound like a $300 studio setup.

Voice Clone AI voice generation

Clone your own voice from a short sample, then generate new narration by typing text. Fix mistakes or add segments without re-recording.

Multi-track Remote recording

Record up to 10 remote guests with separate audio tracks per person. Studio-quality 48kHz recording, no dropouts even on bad connections.

Auto-publish One-click distribution

Publish directly to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Google Podcasts, and 8+ platforms. RSS feed management handled automatically.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Zero setup — record from any browser on any device
  • Magic Dust AI enhancement genuinely transforms bad audio
  • Voice cloning saves hours of re-recording

Verdict

Podcastle is the best "just works" podcasting tool in 2026. If you're starting a podcast or want to simplify your existing workflow, the browser-based recording + AI editing + one-click publishing pipeline is unbeatable for speed. For deeper editing, pair it with Descript.
