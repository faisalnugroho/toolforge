The new naturalness leader for real-time voice agents

Sonic 3.6 (released August 18, 2026, stable snapshot sonic-3.6-2026-08-27) is Cartesia’s latest real-time text-to-speech model, built on state-space-model architecture rather than transformers. It now holds #1 on both Artificial Analysis speech leaderboards — 1,283 Elo on Provider Voice and 1,123 on Controlled Voice — where the Controlled board clones every model onto the same eight reference voices, isolating the synthesis engine from the voice catalog. Listeners preferred it in up to 93% of blind head-to-head tests across fifteen locales. It generates at 136.1 characters per second (vs 46.7 for ElevenLabs’ Eleven v3), supports 44 languages with 61 locales including new Odia and Urdu, handles Hinglish code-switching in a single generation, and preserves speaker accents better on instant clones. At roughly $49 per 1M characters on the Cartesia platform it is the priciest of the arena top three — but still about half of Eleven v3’s $100.

Who it's for: Voice-agent builders, IVR teams, and real-time conversational products that need sub-90ms first audio.

Verdict

Sonic 3.6 is the current quality leader for production voice agents: dual-arena #1, 3x Eleven v3’s generation speed, and demonstrably better cloning and code-switching. If your product talks to customers in real time, it is the model to beat — just price the per-character math against volume, and keep a cheaper model on fallback for non-critical utterances.
