The model OpenAI says entered the AGI era

GPT-6 Astra was released September 3, 2026 as a limited preview, with stable public release September 4 — succeeding GPT-5.6 Sol as OpenAI's frontier flagship. It tops computer use, coding, and math benchmarks: on SRE-Bench (reverse-engineering binaries without source) it solves 88.0% in one attempt versus Sol's 55.9%, and on contamination-controlled ExploitBench it scores 39.0% against Sol's 5.5%. OpenAI says its cybersecurity capability crosses the Critical threshold under the Preparedness Framework, triggering internal security measures. API pricing is $10 per million input and $50 per million output — 2.5x Sol, and exactly matching Claude Fable 5.1.

Who it's for: Teams that need the absolute frontier on computer-use agents, hard reasoning, and security research — and can pay 2.5x Sol's rates for it.

Key features

🖥️ State-of-the-art computer use

Tops the computer-use benchmarks at launch — "Anything you can do on a computer, Astra can do for you" is the pitch, and independent arena results back the claim.

🔐 Cybersecurity above Critical

Scores 39.0% on contamination-controlled ExploitBench (vs 5.5% for Sol) and 88.0% one-shot on SRE-Bench — capability that crossed OpenAI's Critical Preparedness threshold and triggered internal security gating at launch.

🧮 Math and reasoning

Leads Terminal-Bench Science 0.1, ARC-AGI-3, and FrontierMath Tier 4 in OpenAI's launch table against GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Fable 5.1, and Claude Opus 5.

🛠️ Everywhere at once

Rolled out to Pro, Enterprise, and Business Premium in ChatGPT Work and Codex day one, then Plus and Business over following days — plus API, Azure, and AWS Bedrock.

📈 10% token efficiency

Artificial Analysis measured Astra using about 10% fewer output tokens than Sol on general-intelligence benchmarks — a partial offset against the 2.5x price step.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Genuinely tops computer use, coding, and math benchmarks — not a marketing-only generation bump
  • Day-one availability across ChatGPT tiers, API, Azure, and AWS Bedrock
  • ExploitBench and SRE-Bench gains are a step change for security and SRE teams
  • Batch/Flex at half rate and $1 cached input soften the premium for async workloads

✗ What doesn't

  • At $10/$50 it is 2.5x GPT-5.6 Sol and the second-most-expensive OpenAI model after gpt-5.6-cyber
  • Independent analysis puts total cost per task roughly 75% higher than Sol despite token-efficiency gains
  • Daybreak Access staged rollout means smaller orgs waited days behind Pro/Enterprise
  • Security capability crossing the Critical threshold means some capabilities are gated at launch

Verdict

GPT-6 Astra is the clearest frontier gap since GPT-4: if you build computer-use agents, do security research, or need the best reasoning money can buy, it is the model to beat in late 2026. But the 2.5x price step over GPT-5.6 Sol is real — the smart money pattern practitioners use is sending the routine 90% of calls to Sol or Terra and reserving Astra for the hard 10%. If your workload doesn't clearly need the frontier, it doesn't need Astra.
