Gemini 3.1 Pro was released February 19, 2026 as the upgraded flagship of the Gemini 3 series. Its verified 77.1% on ARC-AGI-2 is more than double Gemini 3 Pro's reasoning performance, and it holds a 2M-token context window — the largest of any frontier model. Google's model card shows it beating Gemini 3 Pro, Sonnet 4.6, Opus 4.6, and GPT-5.2 on Humanity's Last Exam (44.4% no-tools, 51.4% with search and code), BrowseComp agentic search (85.9%), and MCP Atlas multi-step workflows (69.2%). Who it's for: Developers analyzing monorepos, long documents, and video in one pass; teams that want frontier-adjacent quality at a fifth of frontier prices.

Key features

📏 2M-token context

The largest context window of any frontier model — roughly 10x Claude — built for whole-codebase analysis, hours of video, and complete regulatory filings.

🧩 Reasoning step-change

Verified 77.1% on ARC-AGI-2, more than double Gemini 3 Pro. Also leads the comparison set on Humanity's Last Exam and BrowseComp in Google's model card.

👁️ Grounded multimodal

Scores 80.5% on MMMU-Pro multimodal understanding and ranks #7 on BenchLM's Multimodal & Grounded category — the strongest tier for screenshots, documents, charts, and grounded workflows.

🤖 Agentic tool use

90.8% on tau2-bench Retail and 99.3% Telecom, with 85.9% BrowseComp — the tool-use backbone behind Google's agent platforms.

$2/$12 per million tokens with $0.20 cached input — a fifth of Fable 5.1 and Astra pricing, and 80.6% SWE-bench Verified for code review at $0.036 per review.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • 2M-token context is unmatched — nothing else comes close at frontier quality
  • Reasoning gains are verified, not marketing: 77.1% ARC-AGI-2, 44.4% HLE no-tools
  • At $2/$12 it undercuts Claude and OpenAI flagships by 4-5x with leading multimodal scores
  • Preview-to-GA path via AI Studio, Vertex AI, and Gemini apps is smooth

✗ What doesn't

  • BenchLM's independent composite scores it #92 of 218 — a mid-table overall rank driven by weaker agentic categories (#115)
  • SWE-bench Verified at 80.6% trails Claude Opus 4.6 (88.6%) and Fable 5 (95.0%) on pure bug-fixing
  • Mid-range 48-61% on LiveCodeBench and SWE-bench Lite in LayerLens's independent 14,549-test run
  • Thinking mode adds latency and token use on every hard call

Verdict

Gemini 3.1 Pro is the value play of the 2026 frontier tier: verified top-tier reasoning and the only 2M-token window, at a fifth of what Anthropic and OpenAI charge for their flagships. It is the default for long-context analysis, multimodal document work, and cost-conscious production. If your workload is pure bug-fixing on code, Claude's Opus/Fable line still holds the SWE-bench crown — but for everything else, 3.1 Pro at $2/$12 is hard to argue with.
