Enterprise AI you can actually own

Granite 4 is IBM's fourth-generation open language model family, tuned for instruction following, RAG, and agentic workflows. The series ships in dense and mixture-of-experts variants, all under Apache 2.0, which makes it a favorite for regulated industries that need auditability and self-hosting.

Who it's for: Enterprises, government teams, and developers who need a fully auditable, self-hostable LLM with strong reasoning and long-context support.

Key features

🧠 128K context window

Process entire codebases or long legal documents in a single pass without chunking artifacts.

📜 Apache 2.0 license

No usage restrictions or field-of-use limitations — deploy commercially with full indemnification from IBM.

🔌 vLLM & Ollama ready

Optimized weights run on vLLM for production or Ollama for local prototyping with minimal config.

🛡️ Enterprise guardrails

Built-in alignment and content-filtering hooks reduce the amount of custom safety middleware you need.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Truly open weights with enterprise backing
  • Excellent long-context recall for RAG
  • Runs efficiently on consumer and datacenter GPUs
  • Clear licensing removes procurement friction

✗ What doesn't

  • Smaller community ecosystem than Llama or Qwen
  • Multilingual depth is narrower outside English
  • Vision and audio variants lag behind flagship models
  • Creative writing can feel stylistically conservative

Verdict

Granite 4 is the pragmatic choice when compliance, auditability, and self-hosting matter more than chasing leaderboard scores.
