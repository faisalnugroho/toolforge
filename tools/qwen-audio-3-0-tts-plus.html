The point-score leader on the independent TTS arena — across 16 languages

Qwen-Audio-3.0-TTS-Plus is the quality-first tier of Alibaba Tongyi Lab’s Qwen-Audio-3.0-TTS release: a hosted text-to-speech model served through Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, not downloadable weights. On the Artificial Analysis Text-to-Speech Arena (July 16, 2026 snapshot) the Plus tier ranks #1 by points at an Elo of 1,237 — its confidence interval overlaps Speechify’s Simba 3.2, so blind listeners rate the two closely — while the real-time Flash sibling trades a little quality for 300ms-level first-packet latency. The family posts the best WER/CER in 10 of 16 supported languages (Flash at 3.87 average, Plus at 3.96), Plus ranks first on speaker similarity across all 16 at 82.75 average, and coverage extends to 20 Chinese dialects. Direction is the headline: fine-grained inline tags steer [whisper], [angry], [breaths], and [laughs] mid-sentence, free-form natural-language control accepts “read this slowly, like a bedtime story”, one-pass long-form runs to 3 minutes, and synthesis stays clean even from noisy reference audio. Flash supports voice cloning; Plus does not in current docs, and voice design from text is unsupported in both. Throughput is the trade: Plus generates at roughly 16 characters per second — quality-first is literal.

Who it's for: Multilingual product teams, e-learning and publishing platforms, and Chinese-market apps that need top-ranked naturalness across 16 languages plus dialect depth.
