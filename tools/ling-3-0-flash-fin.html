A finance specialist on a free flash-class chassis

Ling 3.0 Flash Fin (released August 27, 2026) is InclusionAI’s finance-tuned variant of Ling 3.0 Flash: the same 124B-total / 5.1B-active MoE, sharpened for real-world investment workflows that require complex multi-step tasks and long-horizon planning and execution, while retaining general reasoning, coding, and mathematics capability. It keeps the 262K context window and tool-calling support (tools and tool_choice, though response_format is not enforced), is available free on OpenRouter as a :free endpoint, and carries the family’s MIT license for self-hosting. Third-party catalog listings price paid variants around $0.06/$0.18 per 1M tokens where offered.

Who it's for: Fintech builders, quant researchers, and analysts prototyping investment-reasoning agents without a model budget.

The honest take

  • Genuinely free to try on OpenRouter

✗ What doesn't

Verdict

If you are building finance agents on a budget, Ling 3.0 Flash Fin is the cheapest credible starting point — free API access, MIT weights for fine-tuning, and a 262K window for long analyses. Validate against your own backtests before trusting the domain tuning; the base Ling 3.0 Flash covers general workloads if the finance angle disappoints.
