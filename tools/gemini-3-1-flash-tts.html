Direction-grade voice control at Google scale

Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS (announced April 15, 2026) is Google’s latest text-to-speech model, built on the Gemini 3.1 Flash family and shipping to the Gemini API, Vertex AI, Google Vids, and free experimentation in AI Studio. Its differentiator is the control model: 200+ inline audio tags — [determination], [enthusiasm], [whispers], [laughs], [nervousness], [aggression], and the rest — plus natural-language style prompts steer style, tempo, tone, and accent at fine grain, and multi-speaker dialog generation happens in a single API call, a practical time-saver for audiobooks, e-learning, and agent scripts. Coverage runs to 70+ languages — more than any arena-cluster rival — and every output carries Google’s SynthID watermark to flag AI-generated audio. Pricing is token-shaped rather than character-shaped: $1.00 per 1M text-input tokens and $20.00 per 1M audio-output tokens, with batch mode at half ($0.50 / $10.00). The free AI Studio tier exists but trains on your data; the paid tier does not.

Who it's for: Developers and enterprises already on Google Cloud, accessibility builders, and multi-language products that need expressive, watermark-safe voice at scale.

Key features

🎙️ 200+ audio tags

Inline tags — [determination], [whispers], [laughs], [awe], [aggression] — plus natural-language style prompts give director-level control over tone, pacing, and emphasis.

👥 Multi-speaker in one call

Dialog generation across multiple speakers in a single API call — audiobooks, e-learning scripts, and agent conversations without stitching per-line audio.

🌐 70+ languages

The broadest language coverage of the 2026 TTS field — localized expressive speech for major markets, with SynthID watermarking on every output.

⚡ Batch at half price

$1 per 1M text-in / $20 per 1M audio-out tokens, and batch mode halves it — $0.50 / $10.00 — for offline dubbing and narration volume.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • 200+ inline audio tags plus natural-language style prompts — the deepest direction control of any major TTS
  • 70+ languages leads the field on breadth
  • Multi-speaker dialogs in one call cut audiobook and e-learning production cost
  • SynthID watermark on every output plus Vertex AI enterprise terms

✗ What doesn't

  • $20 per 1M audio tokens runs well above per-character rivals on long content
  • Tag-driven control is prompt-shaped — the arena scores its raw naturalness below the Simba/Qwen cluster
  • Free tier data-use terms differ from the paid tier — read them before shipping product audio
  • Google-stack coupling: the best terms live on Vertex AI and Workspace tools

Verdict

Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS wins on direction and breadth: 200+ audio tags, natural-language style control, multi-speaker dialogs in one call, and 70+ languages — the widest coverage in the field — with SynthID watermarks and enterprise terms on Vertex. Choose it when you need expressive, controllable, multi-language speech inside the Google stack; choose arena-per-naturalness leaders like Simba 3.2 or Qwen Plus when raw blind-listener quality or per-character price decides, and use batch mode to halve the token bill on offline volume.
