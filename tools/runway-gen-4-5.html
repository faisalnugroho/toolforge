Verdict

Gen-4.5 is the proof that model generations age into value. It arrived with receipts — independent wins over Google and OpenAI — then spent a year in production while Runway shipped Gen-5 above it. The result is exactly what a smart buyer wants: cinematic motion, real physics understanding, I2V with characters that stay consistent, native audio, 4K, and a flat $0.12/second API rate on a platform with real editing tools around it. If you need the absolute fidelity ceiling, price Gen-5 or Veo 3.2; if you need reliable, controllable, cinematic video at a price you can budget per second, Gen-4.5 is still the workhorse to beat.
