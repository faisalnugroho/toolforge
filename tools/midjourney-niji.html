When regular Midjourney is too Western

Midjourney Niji is the specialist anime/manga model co-developed by Midjourney and Spellbrush. While base Midjourney excels at photorealism and Western illustration, Niji is fine-tuned entirely on Japanese animation aesthetics — cel shading, vibrant hair colors, chibi proportions, manga linework, and the full stylistic range from Studio Ghibli warmth to modern shōnen action. Triggered with the `--niji 6` parameter (or dedicated Niji mode on web).

Who it's for: Anime/manga artists, webtoon creators, VTubers needing avatars, fan art communities, light novel illustrators, and anyone whose aesthetic leans East Asian illustration.

Key features

--niji 6 Dedicated anime model

A separately trained checkpoint, not a prompt hack. Cel-shading, linework, and anime proportions are baked in — no fighting against photorealism bias.

Stylize Manga to Ghibli range

From classic manga ink-and-screentone to Makoto Shinkai skies to 90s retro OVA aesthetics — one parameter shift changes entire stylistic eras.

Chibi Character merchandise mode

Built-in chibi/super-deformed generation that actually works. Anime artists use this for sticker packs, plushie mockups, and convention merch designs.

Web + Discord Dual interface

Works in both the Midjourney website gallery and the original Discord bot. Parameter syntax identical across both; copy prompts between them freely.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Far ahead of base Midjourney for anime styles — not even close
  • Color theory that actually matches anime conventions
  • Handles famous stylistic references incredibly well (prompt "in the style of")
  • Same subscription as regular Midjourney — no extra cost
  • Active community sharing prompts, LoRAs, workflow tips

✗ What doesn't

  • Worse than base Midjourney for photorealism — use the right tool for the job
  • No fine-tuned LoRA upload like Stable Diffusion
  • Occasionally over-sexualizes female characters (industry-wide anime AI problem)
  • Requires full Midjourney subscription — can't buy Niji separately

Verdict

If your aesthetic is anime/manga, Niji is the single best AI image tool on the market — no contest. Stable Diffusion + anime LoRAs comes close but requires 10x the setup effort. For anime work specifically, just pay the $10 and use Niji.
