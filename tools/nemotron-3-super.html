The mid-size Nemotron built for agent swarms

Nemotron 3 Super is NVIDIA's March 2026 answer for complex multi-agent applications: a 120B-total, 12B-active hybrid of Mamba-2 and Transformer layers with a Mixture-of-Experts router on top. It is the model NVIDIA positions between Nano's efficiency floor and Ultra's frontier push — optimized for collaborative agents, IT automation, and cybersecurity triage where many models must reason at once without latency collapsing.

Who it's for: Teams running fleets of collaborating agents — software development, cybersecurity triaging, IT ticket automation — who want open weights, a 1M-scale context option, and the price competition of a dozen inference hosts.

Key features

🧩 Hybrid Mamba-Transformer MoE

120B total parameters with only 12B active per token: Mamba-2 layers for linear-cost long sequences, Transformer attention where precision matters, MoE routing across the rest.

🚀 Multi-token prediction

MTP delivers over 50% higher token-generation throughput than leading open models per NVIDIA — the difference compounds hard in agent swarms that write a lot.

🔓 Latent MoE economics

Latent experts let the model call 4 experts for the inference cost of 1, improving generalization without paying 4x at serve time.

🎯 1M-scale agent context

262,144-token context on OpenRouter, with NVIDIA publishing a 1M-token window for long-term agent coherence, cross-document reasoning, and multi-step planning via NIM.

📦 Open weights, 12+ hosts

Released with weights, datasets, and recipes under the NVIDIA Open License — served by Baseten, Cloudflare, CoreWeave, DeepInfra, Fireworks, Together, Modal, Nebius and more, fine-tunable with Unsloth.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Leading open-model scores on AIME 2025, TerminalBench, and SWE-Bench Verified after multi-environment RL across 10+ environments
  • Free :free endpoint on OpenRouter to test before spending anything
  • A dozen competing inference hosts keeps pricing honest ($0.09/$0.45 at the low end)
  • Truly open: weights, datasets, and recipes for customization and secure on-prem deployment

✗ What doesn't

  • 120B-class self-hosting needs serious GPU capacity compared to Nano
  • Not a frontier match for closed flagships on the hardest reasoning
  • The free OpenRouter tier logs prompts and outputs — unsuitable for sensitive production traffic

Verdict

Nemotron 3 Super is the open-model sweet spot for agent fleets: Super-class reasoning, 12B-active serving economics, a context window built for long-horizon coherence, and enough competing hosts that you are never locked in. Start on the free endpoint, benchmark it against your incumbent, and self-host the day data sovereignty demands it.
