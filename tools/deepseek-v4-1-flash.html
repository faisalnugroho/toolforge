The new center of gravity of the DeepSeek API

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash went live September 10, 2026 as deepseek-flash — and it is not a side release, it is a takeover. It is the smallest model of DeepSeek new Causal Encoder-Decoder architecture family: a 552B-parameter backbone with only 8B activated per token during prefill and 16B during decode, native image-and-text input, a 1M-token context window with up to 384K output, thinking and non-thinking modes, tool calls, and both OpenAI Responses-format and Anthropic-format API compatibility. DeepSeek states it comprehensively surpassed V4 Pro on performance, cost, speed, and total time — and is backing that claim with forced migration: from noon Beijing time on September 14, every request to deepseek-v4-pro routes to V4.1 Flash at Flash prices. V4 Flash and the Flash vision preview are already retired. Early benchmark reporting puts it eclipsing models like GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 5 on key tests at a fraction of the price.

Who it's for: Cost-sensitive API builders, batch-workload pipelines, and self-hosters who want DeepSeek cheapest capable model with vision

Key features

🏗️ Causal Encoder-Decoder

First of the new architecture family: 552B backbone parameters, 8B activated per token during prefill and 16B during decode — a very different efficiency profile from the V3/V4 dense line.

👁️ Native vision input

Image and text go in natively — no separate vision-preview model. Thinking and non-thinking modes, tool calls, and structured outputs are supported in both.

🕐 Off-peak pricing

$0.15 in / $0.60 out per 1M tokens off-peak, doubling to $0.30/$1.20 in peak windows (01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC, weekdays). Cache hits cost $0.003 off-peak / $0.006 peak per 1M.

🔄 The V4 Pro takeover

From September 14, 2026, deepseek-v4-pro requests route to V4.1 Flash and are billed at Flash prices — a forced price cut for every legacy V4 Pro user, with no V4.1 Pro date announced.

🔓 MIT weights again

Weights published on Hugging Face under MIT for self-hosting, continuing the DeepSeek open-weights tradition that made V3 and R1 ubiquitous.

🔌 Dual API formats

Serves both OpenAI Responses-format and Anthropic-format requests — drop-in for stacks built on either ecosystem, plus tool calling and batch support.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Aggressively cheap: $0.15/$0.60 off-peak with $0.003 cache hits — near the floor of capable API pricing
  • Comprehensively surpasses V4 Pro per DeepSeek, then replaces it at Flash prices

Verdict

V4.1 Flash is DeepSeek doing what it does best — collapsing the price of competence. It beats the model it replaces, inherits its traffic by force, and lands at an off-peak rate ($0.15/$0.60 with $0.003 cache hits) that makes high-volume pipelines viable at budgets nobody else touches. The September 14 V4 Pro cutover is the story: thousands of workloads are getting a price cut and a model swap on the same day, so regression-test your prompts. If you build agents or batch jobs and have not looked at DeepSeek since R1, this is the release to look at.
