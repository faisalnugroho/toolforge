Speech-to-speech that reasons while speaking — 0.70s to first audio

Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 is xAI's second-generation speech-to-speech model, launched July 29, 2026. It topped the Artificial Analysis speech-to-speech quality index at 82.9% (vs 75.7% for version 1.0, 79.1% for GPT-Realtime-2.1 High, and 69.5% for Gemini 3.1 Flash High), hit 0.70-second time to first audio against Gemini 3.1 Flash's 2.98, and scored 56.5% on the agentic tau-voice benchmark. Who it's for: Developers building production voice agents — phone ordering, support lines, booking flows — who need sub-second responsiveness and reliable mid-call tool use.
