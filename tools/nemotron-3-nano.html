The efficiency floor of the Nemotron 3 family

Nemotron 3 Nano is NVIDIA's smallest open agent model: 30 billion total parameters with just 3 billion active per token, trained from scratch for reasoning, tool calling, and assistant workflows. It is the model NVIDIA points at software debugging, summarization, and information retrieval when inference cost is the constraint — and it landed as the family's opening move in December 2025.

Who it's for: Developers building high-volume agents, RAG pipelines, and assistant workflows who want agentic capability at the lowest hosted cost — or full sovereignty by self-hosting on their own GPUs.

Key features

🧠 Unified reasoning switch

One model, two behaviors: a chat-template flag turns the reasoning trace on for hard tasks or off for instant answers — no separate reasoning/non-reasoning checkpoints to juggle.

🚀 4x faster than Nemotron 2 Nano

NVIDIA reports 4x higher throughput than the previous Nano generation; on DeepInfra it serves a P50 of 93.7 tokens/sec with 0.45s time-to-first-token.

📦 Fully open stack

Weights (BF16 and FP8), training datasets, and the RLHF GenRM recipe are all published on Hugging Face — inspect, fine-tune, or deploy anywhere.

💰 $0.09 blended per 1M tokens

$0.05 input / $0.20 output on OpenRouter and DeepInfra, with an 80%-cache-hit input discount — RAG-friendly pricing where long, cheap inputs dominate.

🎯 Agentic plumbing included

262,144-token context, native JSON mode, and function calling make it a drop-in brain for retrieval, triage, and tool-routing agent loops.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Cheapest agentic-class open model per token — $0.05/$0.20 per 1M with a free OpenRouter endpoint
  • Switchable reasoning traces in one checkpoint instead of two models
  • Genuinely open: weights, datasets, and post-training recipes published
  • 262K context at nano pricing beats most small-model windows

✗ What doesn't

  • 30B class will not match Nemotron 3 Super or Ultra on hard multi-step reasoning
  • Strongest in English and coding plus six European languages — thinner multilingual coverage
  • Hosted availability is concentrated in a few providers; self-hosting 30B still needs a real GPU

Verdict

If your product fires thousands of cheap agent calls a day — triage, routing, summarization, retrieval — Nemotron 3 Nano is the current efficiency floor: open weights, a free endpoint to prototype on, and sub-$0.10 blended cost per million tokens at production. When calls get hard, escalate to Nemotron 3 Super or Ultra rather than asking Nano to punch above its weight class.
