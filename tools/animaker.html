Animated explainer video at template speed

Animaker is the DIY animation platform for teams that need character-driven explainer and training videos but cannot hire an animator. Build custom characters, drag scenes into a timeline, and export up to 2K quality on Pro. The 2026 AI additions - script-to-video generation credits on paid plans and AI-built props - push it from template tool toward a genuine AI video workflow.

Who it's for: L&D teams, teachers, startups, and SMB marketers producing training and explainer videos on a template budget.

Key features

🎨 Drag-and-drop animation

A character-and-scene editor with expressions, lip-sync, and camera moves - the classic Animaker workflow that needs zero animation experience.

🤖 AI video generation

Paid plans include monthly AI video generation credits (200 per month on Starter) to draft clips from a script without storyboarding.

📦 Character builder

Build custom animated characters - from 5 per month on Basic up to 30 on Pro - with a stock library of templates and props behind them.

📈 Up to 2K exports

Pro exports 2K-quality video with 30 premium downloads per month plus version history; vertical and square formats are built in for social.

🌐 Social-ready formats

One-click resize across horizontal, vertical, and square cuts for YouTube, Shorts, Reels, and TikTok from the same project.

💸 Free tier to start

A watermarked free plan lets you learn the editor and test characters before paying; annual billing starts around $15/mo.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Character builder with expressions and lip-sync
  • Vertical and square exports built for social
  • AI video generation credits on paid plans
  • Free tier to learn the editor before paying

✗ What doesn't

  • Commercial rights below Pro are ambiguous - verify before client work
  • Export overages and premium assets can push real costs to 2-5x the sticker price
  • 2K export ceiling - no 4K option

Verdict

The fastest route from script to animated explainer for non-designers. Price the export limits honestly - heavy users land on Pro or pay overages - and it beats agency rates ($1,000-5,000 per finished minute) on the first video.
