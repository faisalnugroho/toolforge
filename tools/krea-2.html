The real-time canvas that renders as you paint

Krea 2 is the 2026 version of the real-time AI canvas that made Krea famous. You sketch a rough shape, type a prompt, and the canvas updates in near-real-time as you draw — no 'generate and wait' loop. Version 2 adds 4K output, video canvas mode, and a much better brush model that understands strokes, not just blobs. It's the closest thing to painting with the AI instead of prompting it.

Who it's for: Concept artists, storyboard artists, and designers who think visually and want to iterate at the speed of drawing — not the speed of a generation queue.

Key features

⚡ Real-time canvas

Paint a stroke, see the AI render it in under a second. Iterate visually, not by re-prompting.

🎬 Video canvas

Same real-time loop, now for short video. Sketch motion, get rendered frames.

🖌️ Stroke-aware brush

Understands pressure, velocity, and direction. A stroke is a stroke, not a mask.

📈 4K output

Upscaled final renders up to 4K for production use.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Fastest iterative loop of any image tool
  • Feels like painting, not prompting
  • Video canvas is genuinely new
  • 4K output for final renders

✗ What doesn't

  • Free tier capped at low resolution
  • Real-time model is still less detailed than Flux Pro
  • Subscription required for production work
  • Learning curve for the brush model

Verdict

If you think in sketches, Krea 2 is the tool that finally matches your brain. For final-render quality, pair it with Flux or Midjourney; for the concept phase, nothing else is as fast.
