More reasoning, much more money

DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813 went generally available on August 13, 2026 - the most low-ceremony flagship launch of the year, announced as a version-string update on the API pricing page rather than a release post. The upgrade is real: the 1.6T-parameter mixture-of-experts model (49B active) scores 53 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, up 8 points from the April 2026 V4 Pro, and BenchLM ranks it #32 of 231 with reasoning as its strongest category. But the bargain changed: the April model's permanent $0.435/$0.87 rate became $1.32/$3.96 at peak, with an August 16 peak/off-peak billing split that brings off-peak cache-miss pricing to $0.66/$1.98 (peak hours double it).

Who it's for: Reasoning-heavy workloads - analysis, planning, hard prompts - where the quality bump justifies a mid-tier rate card

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Top-tier reasoning quality at a mid-tier price
  • 1M-token context on a proven MoE architecture
  • Off-peak and cache-hit rates keep real-world costs well below list
  • Open-weights lineage offers a future self-host path

✗ What doesn't

  • 3.6x list-price hike over the April V4 Pro for 8 benchmark points
  • Peak-hour billing doubles the rate during two daily windows
  • Staged silent launch - no official announcement page or model card

Verdict

The reasoning-per-dollar math still favors V4 Pro 0813 if your workload is genuinely reasoning-heavy and you can schedule around peak windows. But this is no longer the budget champion the April model was - cost-first batch workloads should look at V4 Flash or the off-peak tier before committing.
