Photoreal image generation, on your own hardware

Nano Banana 2 is Google's 2026 on-device image model — a successor to the original 'Nano Banana' that went viral for phone-native generation. Version 2 keeps the fully-offline, privacy-first posture but adds character consistency (keep the same face/object across images), batch editing, and a dramatic jump in photorealism. It runs on modern phones and laptops with no API call, no watermark, no cost.

Who it's for: Creators who need consistent characters across a storyboard, privacy-sensitive work (medical, legal, kids), and anyone tired of per-image API pricing.

Key features

📱 On-device

Runs on a phone or laptop with no cloud round-trip. Your prompts and images never leave the machine.

👤 Character consistency

Lock a face or object and re-use it across an entire set — the #1 feature request from the original.

🖼️ Batch editing

Apply the same edit (lighting, background, style) to 50 images at once.

🔒 Zero data training

No upload, no logging, no training on your content. True local-only.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Completely free and private, forever
  • Character consistency that rivals cloud tools
  • Works offline — planes, remote shoots, low-connectivity
  • Batch edit a whole photoset in one pass

✗ What doesn't

  • Lower max resolution than Midjourney or Flux Pro
  • Needs a modern device (2024+ SoC) to be fast
  • Style range narrower than Midjourney's art presets
  • Advanced features still rolling out platform-by-platform

Verdict

The most important image tool of 2026 for anyone who cares about privacy or cost. It's not yet a Midjourney replacement for fine-art work, but for product shots, character-consistent storyboards, and high-volume editing it wins on price and control alone.
