The model that put Meta back in the frontier race

After more than a year of quiet since Llama 4, Meta reorganized its AI efforts under Meta Superintelligence Labs and re-entered the frontier race with Muse Spark in April 2026. It is a natively multimodal reasoning model with support for tool use, visual chain of thought, and multi-agent orchestration — and the first Meta frontier model that is not released as open weights. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it scores 52, placing it in the top five benchmarked models at launch, ahead of Claude Sonnet 4.6, GLM-5.1, MiniMax-M2.7, and Grok 4.20, behind only Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT-5.4, and Claude Opus 4.6.

Who it's for: Meta AI users who want a frontier-class reasoner for free, and teams benchmarking the post-Llama Meta lineup

Key features

⚡ Frontier-class reasoning

Scores 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — top 5 at launch, behind only Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT-5.4, and Claude Opus 4.6. Natively multimodal with text and vision input, text output.

🧠 Contemplating mode

Orchestrates multiple agents that reason in parallel, competing with the extreme reasoning modes of Gemini Deep Think and GPT Pro — 58% on Humanity's Last Exam and 38% on FrontierScience Research.

👁️ Second-best vision

80.5% on MMMU-Pro, behind only Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview. Strong on reasoning and instruction-following: 39.9% on HLE, 5th in CritPT with 11%.

💡 Token efficiency

Used 58M output tokens to run the Intelligence Index — comparable to Gemini 3.1 Pro and notably lower than Claude Opus 4.6 at max effort (157M) or GPT-5.4 xhigh (120M).

🔌 Tool use & agents

Natively supports tool use and multi-agent orchestration. Muse Spark 1.1 (July 2026) adds major gains in tool and computer use, coding, and multimodal understanding.

📱 Everywhere in Meta

Integrated into Meta AI and inside Facebook, Instagram, and Threads — frontier reasoning for free inside apps a billion people already use.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Frontier-class intelligence for free inside Meta AI, Facebook, Instagram, and Threads
  • Contemplating mode parallel-agent reasoning hits 58% on Humanity's Last Exam
  • Second-most capable vision model benchmarked (80.5% MMMU-Pro)
  • Notably token-efficient for its intelligence level

✗ What doesn't

  • Not open weights — a first for Meta, and a break from the Llama tradition
  • No public API at launch; agentic scores trail Claude Sonnet 4.6 and GPT-5.4 on GDPval-AA
  • Availability limited to Meta first-party products for now

Verdict

Muse Spark is the most important Meta model launch since Llama 4 — not because it tops every benchmark, but because it marks the pivot from open-weights research lab to closed frontier competitor with real distribution. If you already live in Meta AI, you get top-5-class reasoning and the second-best vision model benchmarked, free. For API-driven agentic workloads, Claude and GPT-5.4 still lead; the agentic gains in Muse Spark 1.1 are closing that gap. Worth benchmarking, not yet worth switching to.
