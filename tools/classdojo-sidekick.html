A free teacher AI assistant that actually fits the classroom

Sidekick is the AI assistant ClassDojo built to reduce teacher busywork. It drafts lesson plans, generates class materials, writes family messages in a warm teacher voice, and creates quick exit tickets for any topic. It runs inside ClassDojo, the classroom-behavior and communication platform teachers already use, and ClassDojo keeps it free for teachers, schools, and districts.

Who it's for: K-12 teachers who want AI help with lesson planning, materials, and family communication without paying for another edtech subscription.

Key features

book Lesson planning help

Draft lesson plans and generate class materials so the 7-10 hours a week teachers spend planning shrinks dramatically.

message Family messages in teacher voice

Turns what you want to say into professional or casual teacher-voiced messages, with Spanish translation for family communication.

check Assessments and exit tickets

Create a quick exit ticket for any topic you are teaching, generated on the spot.

heart Trusted platform safety

Built with Claude inside ClassDojo, a COPPA-compliant education platform teachers and parents already trust.

zap Free for schools

Free for individual teachers, whole schools, and districts — no per-seat AI pricing.

clock Saves hours weekly

Teachers report reclaiming planning time and getting unstuck on full days when wording would otherwise stall them.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Completely free for teachers, schools, and districts
  • Lives inside ClassDojo, a platform schools already trust and use daily
  • Built with Claude — strong drafting quality for plans and messages
  • Spanish translation for family messaging built in

✗ What doesn't

  • Only useful if your classroom already runs on ClassDojo
  • Focused on K-12 workflows; not a fit for corporate training
  • DRAFTS still need teacher review — outputs are starting points, not finished plans
  • Fewer deep features than paid teacher suites like MagicSchool

Verdict

Sidekick is the lowest-friction AI entry point for a K-12 teacher: free, inside a trusted platform, and genuinely useful for the highest-drain tasks (planning, materials, family messages). If your school uses ClassDojo, there is no reason not to try it; power users will still want a paid suite for heavier generation workflows.
