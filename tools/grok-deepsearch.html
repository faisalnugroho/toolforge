Research that watches the news happen

Grok DeepSearch is xAI's answer to ChatGPT Deep Research -- give it a question and it fans out across the web, reading sources, comparing claims, and composing a cited report. The differentiator is the data pipe: DeepSearch pulls from the live X fire hose as well as the indexed web, so for breaking news, cultural sentiment, or anything moving by the hour, it surfaces material the static-web competitors simply miss. Chain-of-thought is visible by default, which makes it the most auditable of the deep-research tools.

Who it's for: Journalists, traders, culture watchers, and anyone whose research question depends on what people are saying right now.

Key features

X Live X integration

Queries the live X timeline -- posts, threads, and trend velocity -- not just the static web.

C Visible chain-of-thought

Every step of the research plan and reasoning is shown, so you can audit the path.

S Source cards

Each claim in the final report links back to a primary source with a date-stamp.

F Follow-up memory

Ask a follow-up and it keeps the prior source pool, avoiding re-search.

G Grok 5 backbone

Runs on xAI's Grok 5 reasoning stack for multi-hop synthesis.

A Auto-refresh

Re-run a saved research query daily to track an evolving story.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Beats every competitor on breaking-news and sentiment research
  • Transparent reasoning beats black-box search-and-summarize
  • Fast -- full reports in under a minute
  • Synthesis quality rivals Perplexity Deep Research

✗ What doesn't

  • Requires the expensive SuperGrok tier -- no cheap path in
  • Heavy reliance on X as a source carries platform bias
  • Academic and paywalled research coverage is weaker than Perplexity or Scite
  • Free tier is essentially non-existent

Verdict

If your research is about the live internet -- politics, sentiment, breaking events, virality -- DeepSearch is a category of one thanks to the X pipe. For academic synthesis or curated-source reports, Perplexity Deep Research and Elicit remain the safer picks. At $40/mo it is priced for professionals, not tinkerers.
