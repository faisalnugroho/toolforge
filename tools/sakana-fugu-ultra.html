Frontier benchmarks by routing to a pool of frontier models

Fugu Ultra from Tokyo-based Sakana AI is the most interesting category-bender of 2026: it is not a single model but a learned orchestration system that coordinates a pool of publicly available frontier models — its technical report describes routing to GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and Claude Opus 4.8 — combining results from 1-3 agents depending on the problem, all behind one OpenAI-compatible API endpoint. Fugu Ultra v2.0, announced September 11, 2026 alongside Fugu Max, posts genuinely striking numbers: #1 on 5 of 8 hard benchmarks including DeepSWE (74.3, ahead of models priced 3-5x higher per token) and Chartography (48.3 versus 27.3 for Claude Opus 5 and 29.5 for Fable 5) — and it hit those scores without Fable 5, Fable 5.1, or GPT-6 Astra even in its pool. Billing is the clever part: you are charged a single rate based on the top-tier model involved, never stacked model fees. Analysts are split on the positioning — the benchmark numbers are real, but you should know what you are buying is orchestration, not a model.

Who it's for: Teams that want frontier-composite performance behind one OpenAI-compatible endpoint and are comfortable with an orchestrator instead of a single model

Key features

🐟 Orchestrator, not a model

Coordinates a pool of public frontier models — the technical report names GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and Claude Opus 4.8 — routing each problem to 1-3 agents and combining results into a single answer.

🏅 Number-one on hard benchmarks

v2.0 tops 5 of 8 hard benchmarks (DeepSWE 74.3, Chartography 48.3, Toolathon, GDP.pdf, SWEFish) and is top-2 on 7 of 8 — without Fable 5, Fable 5.1, or GPT-6 Astra in the pool.

💰 No stacked model fees

Charged a single rate based on the top-tier model involved: $5 in / $30 out per 1M up to 272K context, $10/$45 above it, cached input $0.50 — multi-agent runs never multiply your bill.

📖 1M context, OpenAI-compatible

1M-token context with 131K max output behind an OpenAI-compatible API — a one-line base-URL switch for existing stacks, with same-day v2 availability.

🇯🇵 Export-control hedge

Sakana positions Fugu as frontier capability without the risk of export controls — a real consideration for global teams navigating US model restrictions.

🐙 A Max sibling too

Fugu Max ($2/$6 per 1M) targets output-per-dollar: best overall on 6 benchmarks including Terminal-Bench 2.1 and GPQA Diamond, with output 40-60% cheaper than Sonnet 5, GPT-5.6 Terra, and Kimi K3.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Composite performance that tops single models on DeepSWE, Chartography, and Toolathon
  • No stacked fees — one rate by top-tier model regardless of agents active
  • OpenAI-compatible, one-line switch, 1M context
  • Fugu Max sibling at $2/$6 is among the cheapest capable endpoints tracked

✗ What doesn't

  • It is an orchestrator of other companies models, not a frontier model of its own — positioning vs reality has drawn fair criticism
  • Less visibility into which worker model handled your request than a single-model API
  • Data retention applies; $10/$45 above 272K context bites on long-doc workloads

Verdict

Fugu Ultra v2.0 is a genuinely novel product: buy one endpoint, get a learned router over a pool of frontier models, and pay one unstacked rate. The benchmark results are real — DeepSWE 74.3 and a 21-point Chartography lead over Opus 5 are not typos — and the economics of never stacking agent fees solve a real multi-agent cost problem. But go in clear-eyed: your requests are served by other companies models selected by a black-box router, which matters for compliance, reproducibility, and vendor story. As a pragmatic endpoint for hard agentic work at predictable cost, Fugu is worth a serious look; as your only model strategy, it is a layer on top of someone else stack.
