Open-weights reasoning catches up

Magistral Medium is Mistral AI's first dedicated reasoning model, trained with reinforcement learning on chain-of-thought traces to handle math, code, and multi-step logic. The Medium tier sits between Mistral's small edge models and their frontier Large series — and unlike the closed o1/o3 family, you can download the weights and self-host.

Who it's for: European enterprises with EU residency requirements, labs that need self-hostable reasoning, and developers building agent pipelines where $15/M token frontier models are too expensive at scale.

Key features

∴ Chain-of-thought reasoning

Trained with RL specifically for reasoning — breaks problems into steps, verifies intermediate results, catches its own slips. On AIME25 math benchmarks it scores ~73% vs o1's ~78%.

Open Apache 2.0 license

Weights are downloadable for commercial use. Self-host with vLLM, llama.cpp, or deploy on Azure AI Foundry. Full auditability — you can trace exactly what it's doing.

EU EU data residency

Backed by Mistral's EU infrastructure — data never leaves the continent. Critical for GDPR-sensitive enterprises and EU government contracts that can't use US-based CLOUD-only models.

Cost ~10x cheaper than o1

$0.40/M input, $2/M output vs o1's $15/$60. For pipelines that chain 10+ reasoning calls per task, that difference decides whether the project is viable or not.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Best price/quality on reasoning benchmarks in the open-weights tier
  • Chain-of-thought is visible — you can audit its logic step by step
  • Apache 2.0 means no surprise ToS rewrites
  • Strong on math, code, and structured multi-step reasoning
  • Multilingual by default — strong on French, German, Spanish reasoning

✗ What doesn't

  • ~5% behind o1 on the hardest math/reasoning benchmarks
  • Smaller context window (128k) vs frontier (200k-1M)
  • No native tool use / function calling parity with OpenAI
  • Slower tokens/sec than non-reasoning models of the same size
  • Fine-tuning ecosystem is younger than Llama's

Verdict

Magistral Medium is the open reasoning model to beat in 2026. If you're EU-based, regulated, or price-sensitive, it's the clear default. If you need the absolute frontier on math or agentic tool use, o1 and Claude still lead — but not by enough to ignore the 10x cost gap.
