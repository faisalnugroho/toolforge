The breadth leader, tightened

GPT-5.5 is OpenAI's late-July 2026 mid-cycle update to GPT-5.4. It's not a new architecture — it's a refinement pass that makes the things GPT-5 already did feel noticeably snappier and more reliable. Chain-of-thought latency is down ~30% on complex reasoning, the multimodal stack parses charts and diagrams more accurately, and Custom GPTs finally hold persistent memory across sessions instead of forgetting everything each Monday.

On our 50-task suite, GPT-5.5 traded blows with Claude Opus 5.2 — winning on breadth, multimodal, and agent reliability, trailing on writing quality and code-review thoroughness. If you live in the OpenAI ecosystem (Custom GPTs, Canvas, voice, plugins), 5.5 is a clear upgrade. If you're choosing fresh, it depends on whether you value breadth (GPT) or depth (Claude).

Who it's for: Generalists, builders who rely on the GPT marketplace, teams that want the widest integration surface, and anyone who wants one AI that does everything competently — even if it's not the best at any single thing.

Key features

Faster CoT Lower-latency reasoning

Chain-of-thought is ~30% faster on complex multi-step problems. You feel it most on hard math, long coding tasks, and deep research — answers arrive in seconds that used to take half a minute.

Multimodal v3 Sharper image/video parsing

Chart, diagram, and table understanding took a visible step up. Financial PDFs, whiteboard photos, and lecture slides now parse with fewer hallucinated numbers.

Custom GPTs 2.0 Persistent memory

Custom GPTs now retain context and instructions across sessions. Build a GPT once with your style guide and it remembers — no more re-pasting your preferences every week.

Canvas+ Collaborative editing

Canvas gets a diff view — see exactly what the model changed before accepting. Makes collaborative editing of long documents genuinely useful instead of a guessing game.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Widest ecosystem — Custom GPTs, plugins, voice, Canvas, agents
  • Excellent multimodal breadth — images, video, audio, documents
  • Custom GPTs with persistent memory are genuinely sticky
  • Best voice mode of any 2026 model — natural cadence and turn-taking
  • Fast reasoning now — the latency improvement is real and noticeable

✗ What doesn't

  • Writing still feels "AI-ish" compared to Claude Opus 5.2
  • Message limits on Plus drain fast with heavy reasoning use
  • Image generation (DALL-E routing) lags Midjourney on aesthetics
  • Incremental over 5.4 — not a must-upgrade if you're happy on 5.4

Verdict

GPT-5.5 is the most complete generalist AI in 2026 — it does everything well, even if Claude Opus 5.2 does writing and code review better. If you're embedded in the OpenAI ecosystem, the faster reasoning and persistent Custom GPTs make 5.5 a clear win. Choosing fresh? Pick GPT for breadth and agents, Claude for prose and analysis. Power users should have both.
