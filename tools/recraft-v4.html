What Recraft V4 does best

Recraft V4 is the 2026 refresh of the design-specialized image model. It generates native SVG vectors alongside raster, locks onto a brand palette from a style reference, and produces UI mockups and marketing visuals that don't scream "AI-generated."

Who it's for: Designers, marketers, and founders who need on-brand visuals faster than Midjourney can be art-directed.
