Nemotron 3 Ultra: 550B params, 55B active, 1M context, Mamba-2 hybrid

Nemotron 3 Ultra is NVIDIA's frontier-scale open model released June 4, 2026 - 550 billion total parameters with 55B active per token, under the permissive OpenMDW-1.1 license, built for agentic reasoning, long-context analysis, tool use and high-stakes RAG. The architecture is the differentiator: a hybrid Latent Mixture-of-Experts (LatentMoE) interleaving Mamba-2 and MoE layers with select attention layers plus Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) for faster generation, trained on 20 trillion tokens with an NVFP4 pre-training recipe. It runs up to a 1M-token context window, serves 11 languages, and NVIDIA reports up to 5x faster inference throughput than comparable models - 300+ tokens/second on recommended infrastructure (minimum 4x GB200/B200 or 8x H100). Reasoning is configurable on/off via the chat template, so you can run it cheap or deep on the same deployment.

Who it's for: Enterprise teams standardized on NVIDIA infrastructure who want an open frontier model with published training data, builders doing long-context analysis and high-stakes RAG, and anyone who needs configurable reasoning rather than always-on thinking.

Key features

🧬 LatentMoE hybrid architecture

Interleaved Mamba-2 and MoE layers with select attention and Multi-Token Prediction - a genuinely different design point from the pure-transformer MoE crowd, tuned for NVIDIA silicon.

📏 1M-token context

Up to 1 million tokens of context for long-document analysis, whole-repo reasoning and high-stakes RAG - with knowledge cutoff September 2025 for grounding.

🧠 Configurable reasoning

enable_thinking=True/False in the chat template - run it as a fast non-reasoning server or a deep reasoner on the same deployment, no model swap.

🌍 11-language multilingual

English, French, Spanish, Italian, German, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese and Chinese - multilingual reasoning as a first-class capability, not a translation bolt-on.

⚡ 5x throughput claim, 300+ t/s

NVIDIA reports up to 5x faster inference throughput vs comparable models, 300+ tokens/second on recommended infrastructure - MTP plus NVFP4 training make speed a feature.

📜 OpenMDW-1.1 + published data

Open weights with commercial use allowed and published training data - the transparency posture most open-weight vendors still avoid.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • OpenMDW-1.1 license allows commercial use and self-hosting with published training data
  • 1M-token context window with configurable on/off reasoning - rare flexibility at this scale
  • Mamba-2 hybrid LatentMoE + MTP architecture is a real design departure, with 5x throughput claims to match
  • 55B active of 550B total - frontier capability at mid-tier serving cost ($0.50/$2.20 hosted)
  • Deep NVIDIA stack integration: NIM, build.nvidia.com ecosystem and first-party hardware guidance

✗ What doesn't

  • Minimum 4x GB200/B200 or 8x H100 to run it - self-hosting is a datacenter commitment, not a homelab one
  • AI Index score of 48 trails the frontier closed models on raw intelligence benchmarks
  • Text-in/text-out only - no vision modality, unlike Qwen3.8-27B or Kimi K3 in the same class
  • NVIDIA-reported benchmarks (5x throughput) await independent replication
  • OpenMDW-1.1 is less widely understood than MIT/Apache - some legal teams will need a read-through

Verdict

Nemotron 3 Ultra is the model you pick when your stack is already NVIDIA-green and you want an open frontier reasoner that respects the hardware it runs on. The hybrid Mamba architecture and configurable reasoning give it a flexibility the pure-transformer crowd lacks, and 1M context with published training data is a serious transparency posture. It is not the smartest model on the board - but at $0.50/$2.20 with 55B active it may be the smartest per-dollar for enterprise RAG and long-context work.
