The first Claude built for multi-day agent sessions

Claude Fable 5.1 shipped September 1, 2026 as Anthropic's most capable model for demanding reasoning and long-horizon agentic work. It keeps Fable 5's 1M-token context window and $10/$50 per-million input/output pricing, but cuts cache-read pricing 75% to $0.25 per million tokens — Anthropic says that trims typical bills about 25% and agentic ones up to 45%. Anthropic's own Finance benchmark runs show it matching Fable 5 accuracy with 20% fewer tokens. Like its sibling Claude Mythos 5.1 (the same model restricted to vetted cybersecurity and life-sciences users), Fable 5.1 is Anthropic's answer to GPT-6 Astra — at an identical $10/$50 price point.

Who it's for: Developers running multi-day autonomous coding sessions, document-heavy finance and legal analysis, and enterprise agentic workflows where cache-read costs dominate the bill.

Key features

⚡ 75% cheaper cache reads

Cache reads dropped from $1.00 to $0.25 per million tokens. Independent analysis of real Claude Code transcripts found cache reads were 67% of list-price cost — the cut made Fable 5.1 about 43% cheaper per turn in practice.

🧠 Long-horizon agentic work

Anthropic positions Fable 5.1 for multi-day autonomous sessions: features spanning an entire codebase, performance work, and self-written tests. Red Hat reported it correctly identified the root cause of every broken build tested.

👁️ Document vision

Understands diagrams, charts, and tables nested in files and PDFs, and uses vision to check its own coding outputs against the original design or goal — built for finance, legal, analytics, and architecture work.

📄 1M context, 128K output

A one-million-token context window with up to 128,000 output tokens handles whole repositories, deal rooms, and regulatory filings in a single pass.

🛡️ EU AI Act watermarking

Following Anthropic's July signature of the EU AI Act Code of Practice on Transparency, models released after August 2 (including Fable 5.1) embed an invisible numerical watermark for provenance checks.

☁️ Everywhere Claude lives

Available on the Claude API and apps plus AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure, with batch mode at half rates ($5/$25) and U.S.-only inference at a 1.1x multiplier.

The honest take

✓ What works

  • Cache-read cut is real money: 67% of typical agentic cost was cache reads, now 75% cheaper
  • 20% fewer tokens than Fable 5 for the same accuracy on Anthropic's Finance benchmark
  • 1M-token context plus vision makes it the strongest Claude for document-heavy work
  • Widely available from day one — API, Claude apps, and all three hyperscalers

✗ What doesn't

  • Headline $10/$50 rates are unchanged and among the most expensive in the market
  • Artificial Analysis measured it spending ~1.7x more output tokens at max effort, so cost per task can rise despite cheaper caching
  • Three compatibility changes from Fable 5 mean existing integrations need replay, shadow traffic, and canary testing
  • The watermark requirement may matter for regulated content workflows

Verdict

Claude Fable 5.1 is the model Anthropic built for work that runs for days instead of minutes. If your bill is dominated by long agent loops that re-read the same context, the 75% cache-read cut alone justifies the switch; if your workload is short uncached prompts, the unchanged $10/$50 rates make Sonnet 5 ($2/$10, now permanent) the smarter buy. For codebases and document piles measured in hundreds of thousands of tokens, nothing else in the Claude lineup holds a candle to it in 2026.
