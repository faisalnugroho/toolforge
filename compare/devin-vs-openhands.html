The verdict

Devin 2 is the polished, supported path: it reliably converts well-scoped tickets into reviewed PRs, with enterprise security and SLAs to match its price. OpenHands matches (and in some SWE-bench-style results exceeds) Devin's autonomy at a fraction of the cost — if you have the infra and the patience to operate it. Startups watching burn should start with OpenHands; enterprises that want a vendor on the hook should pay for Devin.

Winner: Devin 2 for managed reliability — OpenHands for value and control
