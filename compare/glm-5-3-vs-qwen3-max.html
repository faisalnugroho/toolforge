The verdict

GLM-5.3 and Qwen3-Max are the two Chinese open-weight flagships of late 2026, and both win their argument differently. GLM-5.3 is a post-training masterclass: the same 743B base as GLM-5.2, but Terminal Bench 3.0 jumped 4.6→28.3% and DeepSWE 46.2→66.9% purely from RL — plus the best published CyberGym score (84.5%) and a 1.3M-token context, at $0.70/$2.20 with 26 OpenRouter providers competing on price. Qwen3-Max is a scale statement: 2.4T parameters (95B active), post-trained for 10+ days of autonomous coding and 500+ turns of chip-design optimization, with a 1M native context.

For agentic coding and security work, GLM-5.3 is the value pick — its gains are independently replicated (GDPval-AA v2 1769, ahead of Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol), its CyberGym score is the best published, and it costs roughly a third of Qwen3-Max’s output price. For the longest autonomous runs where sheer parameter scale matters, Qwen3-Max’s cowork post-training shows real long-horizon discipline — the 10+ day coding runs are what you pay the premium for.

Our pick: GLM-5.3 for most builders — cheaper, benchmark-best in agentic/security, MIT-licensed; Qwen3-Max for maximal long-horizon autonomy at scale.

Winner: GLM-5.3 for value and agentic/security benchmarks; Qwen3-Max for long-horizon autonomous scale
