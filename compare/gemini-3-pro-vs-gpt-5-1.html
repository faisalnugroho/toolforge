The verdict

Use Gemini 3 Pro if you need a massive 2M-token context window, Google Workspace integration, native multimodal analysis (video, audio, images), and the best free tier. Use GPT-5.1 if you want the best reasoning capabilities, built-in image generation (DALL-E), voice mode, plugins, and the largest third-party ecosystem. Both cost $20/mo — power users should have both.

Winner: Tie — depends on workflow

How we tested

Both models were tested for 4 weeks across 50+ real-world tasks: coding challenges, long-form writing, multimodal document analysis, research synthesis, and creative generation. Where Gemini 3 Pro wins

Massive context: Gemini 3 Pro's 2M-token context window is 15x larger than GPT-5.1's 128K. If you need to analyze entire codebases, 1,000-page documents, or hours of video, Gemini is the clear choice. We fed it a 500-page legal contract and it found specific clauses faster and more accurately than GPT-5.1 could with its limited context.

Google Workspace integration: Gemini connects natively to Gmail, Google Docs, Drive, and Calendar. You can ask "summarize emails from my boss this week" or "what's in my Drive about Project X?" — GPT-5.1 can't match this.

Native video understanding: Gemini can watch and analyze video directly, understanding motion, actions, and spoken content. GPT-5.1 processes frames but lacks true temporal video understanding.

Where GPT-5.1 wins

Reasoning depth: GPT-5.1's o-series reasoning mode is still unmatched for complex multi-step problems. We tested it on math competitions, logic puzzles, and code debugging — GPT-5.1 consistently produced more thorough, accurate reasoning chains.

Image generation: DALL-E is built in. You can generate images, edit them, and use them in your work without switching tools. Gemini requires a separate Imagen call.

Ecosystem: ChatGPT's plugin marketplace, GPT Store, and third-party integrations dwarf Gemini's ecosystem. If you use AI with other tools, GPT-5.1 connects to more of them.
