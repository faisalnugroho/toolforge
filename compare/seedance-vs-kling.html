The verdict

Use Seedance 1.5 if you want cinematic photorealism and tight camera control at the cheapest per-clip price. Use Kling 3 if your work depends on realistic humans in motion — dancing, fighting, gymnastics — or if you need Kuaishou's battle-tested API stability. Both beat Sora and Runway on price.

Where Seedance wins

Cinematic scenes and price. Seedance's 2026 1.5 release is a clear step up on landscape shots, product hero shots, and any scene where lighting does the storytelling. The camera-control vocabulary (dolly-in-with-orbit, crane-over-shoulder, etc.) is broader than Kling's. It's also about half the price per clip — at 1,000 clips/month the difference is $50 vs $100.

English prompt adherence is noticeably tighter. Kling 3 was trained heavily on Chinese and translates through an internal step; subtle English phrasing occasionally gets missed. Seedance handles compound English sentences about lighting, mood, and camera work more reliably.

Where Kling wins

Human motion. Hands, fingers, dance, martial arts, gymnastics — Kling 3 has been the leader here since 2024 and Seedance hasn't caught up on this axis. Our panel rated Kling ~10% higher on motion-naturalness for human subjects. If your content is human-driven (fashion, fitness, sports, dance), Kling is still the safer pick.

API maturity. Kuaishou's API has been in production for 18 months — documented rate limits, SDKs in three languages, and reliable SLA. ByteDance's Seedance API is newer and we've seen more 5xx blips in load tests. For mission-critical pipelines, that gap matters more than per-clip cost.

How we tested

We generated 60 matching-prompt clips on each platform across six categories: cinematic landscape, product hero, human motion, action, portrait narration, and abstract. Three human raters scored realism, prompt adherence, and artifact rate blindly. We also measured generation time and API error rates across a 24-hour soak. See our full disclosure.
