The multilingual champion

Qwen3-Max is the model that put Alibaba on the frontier map. With 1M-token context, support for 29 languages with native fluency, and a mixture-of-experts architecture that keeps costs low, it's a serious alternative to GPT-5 for teams that need non-English language support or long-context document processing.

Where Qwen3-Max wins

  • ✓ 3x cheaper — $0.40/M input vs $1.25/M, $1.20/M output vs $10/M
  • ✓ 2.5x context — 1M tokens vs 400K for GPT-5
  • ✓ Asian languages — Best-in-class Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Southeast Asian language support
  • ✓ Math & coding — Competitive on AIME, MATH-500, and LiveCodeBench

Where GPT-5 wins

  • ✓ Multimodal — Vision, audio, and video understanding that Qwen3-Max lacks
  • ✓ More languages — 95+ vs 29, including low-resource languages
  • ✓ Agent reliability — Superior function calling and structured outputs
  • ✓ Ecosystem — Universal framework and platform support

Verdict

If your work involves Asian languages or you need 1M-token context at a low price, Qwen3-Max is the clear winner. For multimodal tasks, broad language coverage, or production agent systems, GPT-5 remains the safer choice. Teams operating in APAC often use Qwen3-Max as their primary model and GPT-5 as a fallback for English-heavy multimodal work.
