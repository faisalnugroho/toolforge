The verdict

If you can get into Fairwind: Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber - the discovery-and-patch loop with CodeMender and Mandiant/Wiz behind it is the most complete security-agent stack any vendor has shipped. If Fugu-Cyber's approval process fits you: the 86.9% CyberGym score plus human red-team in one package is a stronger published benchmark position - but both numbers are vendor-run and un-replicated, so benchmark the public alternatives first. Most teams should treat these two as the gated frontier and start with GPT-5.6-Cyber (Daybreak) or CodeMender for the access timeline alone.

Winner: Neither is purchasable without vetting, so the real question is which gate you can get through. Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber wins on ecosystem - autonomous discovery-to-patch inside the full Google threat-defense stack with CodeMender diffs landing in VS Code. Fugu-Cyber wins on the published number: 86.9% CyberGym is the best on the benchmark, and the bundled human expertise is a delivery model pure API vendors can't match.
