The verdict

If your workload is mostly classification, extraction, and high-volume chat with vision, GPT-4o mini's price is unbeatable. If you're doing agentic coding, long-doc Q&A, or anything where the model has to follow a careful procedure, Haiku 4.5 is worth the 5x price — it produces fewer retries and cleaner structured output, which often nets out cheaper on total tokens spent.

Where Haiku 4.5 wins

Haiku 4.5 punches way above its weight on coding. In our tests on real repo diffs, it produced correct, clean patches 71% of the time — compared to 52% for GPT-4o mini. On long-doc Q&A, the 200k context means you can feed whole PDFs without chunking, and its instruction following is meaningfully more reliable: when you tell it "respond only in JSON matching this schema," it does.

Where GPT-4o mini wins

GPT-4o mini is a volume workhorse. At $0.15/Mtok input, you can run classification on a million documents for the cost of a nice dinner. Its vision support is excellent (better than Haiku's), and OpenAI's ecosystem — batch API, assistants, fine-tuning — is more mature. For a chatbot that handles a million customers, mini is the obvious default.

How we tested

We ran 500 production-style prompts through both APIs over two weeks: 200 classification tasks, 150 extraction tasks, 100 coding diffs, and 50 long-doc Q&As. Same prompts, same temperature (0), scored blind by two reviewers on accuracy, format compliance, and usability. Pricing is as of July 2026.

Both are strong picks. Start with the one that fits your stack.
