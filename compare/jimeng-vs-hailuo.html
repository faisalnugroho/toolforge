The verdict

Use Jimeng if you want an all-in-one mobile workflow — generate, edit, caption, publish — without leaving ByteDance's ecosystem. Use Hailuo if you need pure output quality, better photorealism per clip, or if you're already stitching clips in CapCut/Descript/Premiere anyway.

Where Jimeng wins

Workflow. Jimeng is a full video editor with a video model inside, not a model with a thin UI on top. You can generate a clip, drop it on a timeline, add captions, transitions, and background music, then publish directly to Douyin or export for TikTok/Reels — all in the same session. For content creators, that bundled flow is worth a lot.

Mobile experience is a clear win. Jimeng's iOS/Android apps are polished, fast, and handle vertical video natively. Hailuo's mobile web is workable but feels like an afterthought. If your workflow lives on your phone, Jimeng is the obvious pick.

Where Hailuo wins

Output quality per clip. Our 50-clip panel rated Hailuo slightly higher on photorealism (8.6 vs 8.2) — it's still the strongest in the short-form AI video tier for realistic skin, hair, and bokeh. Subject Reference (character consistency across clips) is also more reliable than Jimeng's character feature.

Hailuo's mature web app and community are worth considering. There are hundreds of shared prompt templates, an active Discord, and a more established credit system. For studios batching dozens of clips per week, Hailuo's web-first workflow integrates better with downstream tools.

How we tested

50 matching prompts across cinematic, product, character, and social categories. Blind panel of three raters scored photorealism, motion naturalness, and prompt adherence. We separately scored workflow features (editor, mobile UX, character tools) hands-on. See our full disclosure.
