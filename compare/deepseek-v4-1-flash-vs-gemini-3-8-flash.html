The verdict

This is the September 2026 flash-model price war in one page. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is the floor-setter: $0.15/$0.60 off-peak with $0.003 cache hits, native vision, a 1M-token window, MIT weights for self-hosting, and benchmark reporting that puts it ahead of models costing 10-20x more per token. Gemini 3.8 Flash is the ecosystem pick: roughly six points better on long-horizon coding than the previous Flash, backed by the Gemini API, Vertex AI, and the integrations that come with living in the Google stack — at an intro $0.75/$3.75 that is cheap for Google but still 5-6x DeepSeek.

Our verdict: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash wins on value. At a 5x input and 6x output discount with open weights as the escape hatch, Gemini 3.8 Flash has to justify itself on capability and ecosystem — and for long-horizon coding workloads and Google-native shops, it can. But for everyone else routing volume through a flash tier, DeepSeek just made the decision arithmetic very simple. Budget one caveat either way: Gemini intro pricing doubles on January 1, 2027, and DeepSeek doubles in peak windows — both flash tiers punish you for not reading the fine print.
