Where each wins

Grok 6 wins on: Real-time information (native X integration — freshest data of any model), fewer guardrails (will engage with topics GPT refuses), personality (genuinely funny and edgy), and native image generation (Aurora) with strong prompt adherence. If you need to know what's happening right now, Grok is the only model that does it well.

GPT-5.4 wins on: Overall polish and quality, voice mode (best in industry), the plugin/agent ecosystem, image generation (DALL-E), and consistent reliability across tasks. GPT-5.4 is the more well-rounded model — it does more things well, even if it's not the best at any single thing.

The verdict

Use Grok 6 if you need real-time information, fewer guardrails, or a model with personality. Use GPT-5.4 if you want the most capable all-around AI with the best ecosystem. GPT-5.4 is the better general-purpose model and costs $10 less. Grok 6 is the specialist — unbeatable for news and trends, but narrower in scope.

Winner: GPT-5.4 for most users, Grok 6 for real-time

How we tested

Both models were tested for 30+ days across 50 real-world tasks: real-time news queries, coding, creative writing, reasoning, and voice interactions. We don't accept payment for reviews — see our full disclosure.
