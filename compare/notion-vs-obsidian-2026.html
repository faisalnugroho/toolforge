Notion and Obsidian are the two most popular note-taking apps of 2026, but they solve fundamentally different problems. Notion is a cloud-based all-in-one workspace — databases, wikis, projects, and now AI, all in one place. Obsidian is a local-first knowledge graph that stores everything as plain Markdown files on your computer.

The question isn't which is "better" — it's which fits your workflow. If you collaborate with a team and want everything in one place, Notion. If you're a researcher, writer, or knowledge worker who values privacy and wants a lifelong second brain, Obsidian.

The verdict

Use Notion if you want an all-in-one workspace with databases, team collaboration, and built-in AI. Use Obsidian if you value local-first privacy, own your data as Markdown files, and want a true knowledge graph. Notion wins for teams; Obsidian wins for power users who want control.

Winner: Notion for teams, Obsidian for power users
