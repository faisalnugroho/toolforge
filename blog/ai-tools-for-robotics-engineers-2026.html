How we picked

Every tool on this list was actively used by at least 5 robotics engineers working on production robots or research. We tested each for 2+ weeks, scored on simulation fidelity, ROS integration, real-world usefulness, and price. We removed anything that was purely academic with no deployment path.

The verdict

Start with ROS 2 + Gazebo — it's the backbone. Add NVIDIA Isaac Sim for simulation and synthetic data. Use MuJoCo for RL. Bring in ChatGPT/Claude APIs for task planning and debugging. LeRobot is the wild card — if you're doing embodied AI, it's the framework to watch.
