What actually changed this month

Reasoning went open. Magistral Medium hitting Apache 2.0 means every regulated industry can now self-host o1-class reasoning for the first time. Expect a wave of EU enterprise deployments in Q4.

Video hit the 10-second native ceiling. Seedance 1.5, Kling 3, and Jimeng all now do 10s at 1080p without stitching. Six-second clips are officially legacy.

Agent research consolidated. Perplexity Labs going GA in Pro ($20) put real pressure on standalone research agents — when your search engine ships an agent runtime for free, paying $40/mo for a separate agent gets hard to justify.
