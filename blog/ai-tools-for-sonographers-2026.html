How we picked

Every tool on this list was actively used for 2+ weeks by the ToolForge editorial team. We scored each on output quality, time saved, ease of use, and price-to-value, then removed anything that wasn't worth the subscription.

The verdict

The pattern across every sonography AI success story in 2026 is the same: AI does not read the study, it removes everything around the study. Guided acquisition (Butterfly, Clarius-plus-ThinkSono) shortens the learning curve for novices and speeds protocol scans; measurement and reporting tools (Claude, Otter) collapse the documentation tail that keeps sonographers after hours. The tools that failed in our testing were the ones promising diagnosis — automated read tools still require verification and carry liability questions no department has resolved. Start with a handheld AI-guided probe if you do mobile or point-of-care work, add a reporting assistant if documentation eats your evenings, and keep the diagnostic AI in the research column until regulation catches up.
