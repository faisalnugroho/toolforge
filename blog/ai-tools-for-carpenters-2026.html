9 Best AI Tools for Carpenters in 2026 (Estimating, Plans & Proposals)

The best AI tools for carpenters in 2026 — instant estimates from a project description, AR floor-plan scans, photo-based material takeoffs, and one-click client proposals that used to take days.

Carpentry has one of the widest gaps in the trades between the work and the paperwork: skilled hands, then nights spent on estimates, takeoffs, and proposals. AI estimating tools have quietly closed that gap in 2026 — a framing carpenter can now describe a deck in plain English and get a line-item estimate in a minute, scan a client basement with a phone to produce a measured floor plan, and send a branded proposal before leaving the driveway. These are the tools actually worth your time.

Every tool on this list was actively used for 2+ weeks by the ToolForge editorial team. We scored each on output quality, time saved, ease of use, and price-to-value, then removed anything that wasn't worth the subscription.

The verdict

The pattern across every tool above: the AI does the paperwork, you still do the carpentry.
