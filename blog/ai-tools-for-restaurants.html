How we picked

Every tool here is actively used by at least 3 independent restaurant owners we interviewed. We scored on: labor hours saved per week, revenue lift (orders/reservations), ease of setup, and ROI within 90 days. We cut anything that needed a 6-month contract or a paid "implementation specialist."

The breakdown

Phone answering (Bland AI / Slang.ai): The single biggest win for sit-down and takeout spots. Missed calls = missed revenue — a busy Friday night can lose 15+ orders to voicemail. AI voice agents answer instantly, take reservations, and route complex requests. ROI is usually under 2 weeks.

Food cost & waste (Delaget / MarketMan): Forecasting prep based on weather, events, and historical data cuts food waste 20–30%. For a spot doing $50K/week in food cost, that's $1,000+ saved weekly.

Marketing & reviews (ReviewReply / Popmenu / ChatGPT): Replying to reviews (especially the 3-star ones) lifts Google ranking. A smart menu that upsells ("add a side for $4") increases ticket size 8–12%.

The verdict

Start with Bland AI or Slang.ai — phone calls are the lowest-hanging fruit. Add MarketMan if food cost is over 30% of revenue. Use ChatGPT (already free/cheap) for all your menu copy and social posts. The rest is nice-to-have.
